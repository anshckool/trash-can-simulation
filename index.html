Aerodynamic Quadratic Drag

Standard parabolic calculations severely overshoot real paper tosses. We integrate true quadratic fluid drag:

F_drag = -0.5 * ρ * C_d * A * |v| * v
• Mass: m = 0.0045 kg (4.5 g)
• Radius: r = 0.035 m (Area = 0.00385 m²)
• Drag Coeff: C_d = 0.80 (crumpled paper)
• Air Density: ρ = 1.225 kg/m³

Result: Paper loses 15%–35% horizontal range and steepens dramatically before reaching the rim plane.
